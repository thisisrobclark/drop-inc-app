### Description

CropShield is the ordering app for CropShield Chemicals Ltd. Browse the full product catalog, place orders, and track your delivery status — all from your phone.

**For Farm Partners:**
- Browse the complete product catalog with categories like herbicides, fungicides, insecticides, seed treatments, and more
- Add products to your cart and submit orders in seconds
- Track your order status from received through to delivered
- View your full order history

**For CropShield Admins:**
- View and manage all incoming orders from the admin dashboard
- Update order tracking status step by step
- See customer details, delivery directions, and order notes

Simple, fast, and built for the field.
